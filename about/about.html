📡
Real-Time RF Telemetry

Extracts RSRP, SINR, RSRQ, RSSI, Cell ID, Bandwidth, and Carrier Aggregation from 4G/5G gateways (Zyxel, Huawei, ZTE, etc.).

⚡
Speedtest Correlation

Automatically pairs speed test results (Speedtest.net, Fast.com) with matching RF signal snapshots.

📊
Hourly Audits & AI Export

Consolidates telemetry into hourly rollups and formatted Markdown diagnostic reports for easy sharing.

🔒
100% Privacy First

All data stays 100% local inside chrome.storage.local. Zero telemetry sent to external servers.
