4x4 MIMO Antenna Cross-Polarization Guide

For external TS9 / SMA antennas (e.g. Poynting, Panorama, QuWireless), ensure dual elements are angled at ±45° (slant cross-polarization) to align with cell tower transmit arrays:

  • Element A (+45°): Primary Downlink/Uplink transceiver array.
  • Element B (-45°): Secondary Downlink diversity receiver.
  • Line of Sight (LoS): Elevate antenna above rooflines to clear tree foliage and building obstacles.
